INTELLIGENT INTERRUPTION HANDLING - DEMO TRANSCRIPT
===================================================

This transcript demonstrates the backchannel filtering feature.
The agent ignores passive acknowledgements (mhmm, okay, yeah) while speaking,
but correctly stops when given actual interrupt commands (stop, wait).


SESSION START
-------------

AGENT: Hello! I'm Alex, and I'm here to tell you some great stories. 
       Would you like to hear a story today? Just let me know what 
       topic interests you!

USER:  Sure.

AGENT: Great! Let me tell you a story about an unexpected adventure 
       that begins in a quiet, ordinary town and leads to extraordinary 
       discoveries.

       In the small town of Willowbrook, nestled between rolling hills 
       and dense forests, lived a curious young girl named Elara. She 
       had always been fascinated by the tales her grandmother told her 
       about ancient times and hidden realms. One day, while exploring 
       the attic of her...


BACKCHANNEL TEST (Agent continues speaking without pause)
---------------------------------------------------------

USER:  Mhmm.          <-- IGNORED (agent kept speaking)
USER:  Okay.          <-- IGNORED (agent kept speaking)  
USER:  Yes.           <-- IGNORED (agent kept speaking)
USER:  Uh-huh.        <-- IGNORED (agent kept speaking)


INTERRUPT TEST (Agent stops immediately)
----------------------------------------

USER:  Stop.          <-- INTERRUPT DETECTED

AGENT: Got it, I'll pause here for a moment. If you want me to continue 
       or switch to a different story or topic, just let me know!


RESPONSE WHEN SILENT (Normal conversation)
------------------------------------------

USER:  Yeah.

AGENT: Alright, picking up from where I left off—Elara was rummaging 
       through the dusty attic filled with forgotten treasures and old 
       trunks. Among the cobweb-covered boxes, she found a...


MIXED INPUT TEST (Contains interrupt word -> Agent stops)
---------------------------------------------------------

USER:  Mhmm. But wait. Stop.    <-- "but wait" triggers interrupt

AGENT: [Stopped and listened]


RESULTS
-------
- Backchannel while speaking: PASSED (agent continued seamlessly)
- Interrupt word while speaking: PASSED (agent stopped)
- Response when silent: PASSED (agent responded normally)
- Mixed input with interrupt: PASSED (agent detected "wait/stop")
